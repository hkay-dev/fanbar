Fanbar's panel has an Energy row with three buttons: Low, Auto and High. They
are the same Low Power, Automatic and High Power modes that you find in System
Settings under Battery, and the one that's on is highlighted when you open the
panel.

## Change the mode

1. Click the fan in the menu bar.
2. In the Energy row, click Low, Auto or High.

The mode changes for the power source the Mac is on right now. macOS keeps one
mode for battery and another for the charger, so picking High while plugged in
leaves the battery mode as it was.

A change made in System Settings while the panel is open shows up the next time
you open the panel.
